## Fewest steps to publish
**Videos (6), takes about 5 minutes on desktop:** open tiktok.com/tiktokstudio/upload, then for each video folder:
1. Upload `video.mp4`.
2. Paste `caption.txt`.
3. Turn **AI-generated content ON**.
4. Choose Schedule and set the time below. You can schedule 15 minutes to 10 days ahead.
5. Post.

If Schedule is missing on your account, post from the app at the times below.

**Photo carousels (3), from the TikTok app (phone):**
1. Save the `slides/` JPGs to your phone.
2. Tap + then Photo and select them in order (slide_01 to slide_07).
3. Add a sound from TikTok's library in-app.
4. Paste `caption.txt` and post at the time below. Leave AI-generated content OFF: photo posts have no AI voice or visuals.
5. Alternative: post `narrated_slideshow.mp4` as a video instead, with `caption_video_version.txt` and AI-generated content ON. Post one or the other, never both.

After posting, record it with `./yv7.sh posted <id> <tiktok-url>` (IDs are in `./yv7.sh queue`).

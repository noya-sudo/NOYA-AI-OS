## The commercial problem (CEO decision required)
Rockstar's policy defines "non-commercial" as **not making money through the game footage you post**.
TikTok Creator Rewards pays per qualified view. Earning Creator Rewards on videos built on GTA VI gameplay is, on a plain reading,
**making money through game footage** — outside the fan policy. Many creators do it and Take-Two rarely acts, but "rarely enforced" is not a lawful basis.

Options:
- **A (recommended):** Request a licence now (draft: `RIGHTS/rights_request_draft.md`). Until answered, enrol in Creator Rewards only once
  eligible and apply it only to videos whose value is dominated by original commentary/graphics, and accept the documented takedown risk on gameplay videos.
- **B:** Treat the account as non-commercial for GTA gameplay; monetise only original-format videos (commentary, graphics, tips without footage).
- **C:** Monetise everything and accept the policy risk (not recommended: takedowns/strikes would hit the main asset — the account).

## Sponsorships
Disabled. No paid brand content on GTA footage until a licence or documented lawful basis exists. Never mix paid sponsorship
into Creator Rewards videos where TikTok prohibits it; label any branded content with TikTok's content disclosure toggle.

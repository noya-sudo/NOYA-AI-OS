## 9. Strengths
1. Coherent dark, restrained visual identity (navy `#061422`, Libre Baskerville + Inter) that reads premium.
2. Membership page copy — confident, specific (48h), understated.
3. Clear service taxonomy that matches the business.
4. Real branded photography exists (robes, towels, cards, events) — rare and valuable.
5. Corporate/brand copy is structured and commercially literate.
6. Legal pages are properly structured (intermediary model, retention periods).
7. Stable, descriptive URL slugs — easy SEO migration.

## 10. Weaknesses
1. **Live legal placeholders** (`[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`, `[JURISDICTION]`, `[REGISTERED ADDRESS]`, `[ENQUIRIES EMAIL]`) on Privacy and Terms — credibility and compliance risk. **Fix before anything else, including on Squarespace.**
2. No direct contact channel (no email, phone, WhatsApp link) — for a concierge business, this suppresses conversion.
3. Broken CTAs: Home "Apply for membership" → `/`; every "Make an Enquiry" self-links; Exclusive Access "Apply For Membership" self-links; footer Explore items unlinked.
4. Homepage SEO title "N O Y A"; 6 pages with empty meta descriptions; no H1 on 13 of 15 pages; one OG image sitewide.
5. Duplicate URLs indexed (`/home`, `/what-we-do-folder`).
6. "Coming soon" labels on live services (What We Do).
7. Image rights exposure: ~32 images from third-party sources, 22 from one agency, 31 screenshots.
8. Header logo is a screenshot PNG; footer logo hot-linked from a different Squarespace site.
9. No proof: no testimonials, case studies, partners, press, numbers.
10. Carousels hide 60%+ of service cards on desktop.
11. Egypt advantage invisible.
12. Cart icon exposed with no store.

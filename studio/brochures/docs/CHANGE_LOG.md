# NOYA Brochure Collection · Editorial Change Log

*From the four 2026 brochures (Canva: NOYA Concierge Brochure, Noya Private Desk, NOYA CORPORATE, Noya Concierge Brands and Productions)
and the Partner Guide (Summer & Autumn 2026) to the 2026-11 collection. The original Canva designs are untouched.*

## Collection-level decisions

1. **Four brochures become five plus a portfolio.** A Trade & Partners brochure replaces the Partner Guide; a separate, quarterly
   Selected Work document carries proof, so the brochures never need re-exporting when new work is added.
2. **One subject, one home.** The old set repeated the same service lists in three or four documents (Corporate even carried a full
   Brands & Productions page). Each topic now lives in one document and is pointed to from the flagship (see ARCHITECTURE).
3. **Evidence before claims.** A mailbox, Drive and database audit (21 Nov 2026) found one completed engagement that can be shown
   (Cairo wedding, Oct 2025) and no support for several printed claims. Unsupported claims were removed, not softened.
4. **Rebuilt in Studio, not Canva.** The collection is generated from version-controlled copy (`content/*.mjs`) with NOYA's house fonts,
   a vector logo and an automatic overflow, link and image-rights check. One contact block and one set of caveats are defined once.
5. **Contact block unified.** Founder, email, WhatsApp, website and Instagram in one consistent, live-linked block on every closing page.

## r2 · Creative director's refinement · 24 Nov 2026

**Photography.** Full archive re-audit (website library, 32 Canva designs, Drive metadata, the 2026 brochures). 39 new high-resolution
photographs registered; low-resolution brochure extracts replaced by their originals where they exist (robes, towels, car flutes, beach

**Typography and design system.** Type scale to the brief (body 10.75 pt, service text 10 pt, labels and small print 8.5 pt, headlines 31 pt,
cover 42 pt); small-print contrast raised; warmer page white; split column widened; balanced headings and pretty wrapping; new cinematic
layout (01 Saint-Tropez); numbered engagement options with aligned inclusions (03 p7); typographic sourcing categories (02 p6).

**Production.** Static font instances (CID TrueType instead of Type 3); separate digital (≤ 3 MB) and print (full resolution, 3 mm bleed)
exports; effective-resolution measurement on every build; before-and-after comparison; previous final PDFs archived as the baseline.

**Copy.** Tightened where the new layouts called for it (01 "who we work with", lifestyle, Egypt, worldwide and how-we-work pages; 04 talent
page condensed for its new photographic layout). "The Alps in winter" added to the worldwide list (NOYA holds signed chalet-operator
agreements and publishes Alpine stays on its website). No new services, claims or engagements.

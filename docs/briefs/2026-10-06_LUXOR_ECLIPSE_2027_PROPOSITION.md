## The moment

- **When and how long.** Monday 2 August 2027. Totality at Luxor runs 13:02:06–13:08:28 local time (EEST), about 6 min 22 s. VERIFIED: NASA, rechecked 6 Oct 2026.
- **Why Luxor.** It sits close to the point of greatest eclipse, and the temples and the Nile give the moment a setting nowhere else has.
- **Elsewhere.** Aswan sees only a partial eclipse. Safaga and Hurghada are partial. Marsa Alam, on the Red Sea, has about 5 min 30 s. VERIFIED (eclipse maps; research 4–6 Oct 2026).
- **Demand.** About 90% of Luxor's inventory is reported gone (CNN). INFERRED from press: treat as "scarce", not as a measured figure.
- **Heat.** Early August in Luxor is routinely above 40 °C, and totality falls at midday. Shade, cooling, water, medical cover and short outdoor windows are part of the product, not extras.

   - Private dinners.

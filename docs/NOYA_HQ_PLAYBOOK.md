# Adam's NOYA HQ Playbook

hq.noyaconcierge.com works on your phone. Use the bottom bar: Today · Replies · Outreach · Search · Menu.

## Daily — 15 minutes (09:00)
1. **Today.** Clear every P1 first: replies, meeting requests and website enquiries.
   - Use **Open email** to reply in Gmail.
   - Use **Record meeting** after any call. Two lines is enough; pick the new stage and HQ books the follow-up.
2. **Outreach → Ready.** Read each email card.
   - **Approve & Draft** creates a Gmail draft. You then press Send in Gmail.
   - Or use **Edit**, **Hold**, **Reject** (with a reason), or **Change channel**.
3. **Outreach → LinkedIn / Instagram.** For each message:
   1. **Copy message.**
   2. **Open LinkedIn**, then paste and send it yourself.
   3. Press **Mark sent**. This closes the task and books one follow-up.
4. **Follow-up tab.** Follow up once, then once more at most, then move the contact to Long term.
5. **Past relationships → They wrote last.** Answer anyone still waiting on you (each item opens the Gmail thread).
6. Glance at **System health**. Green means nothing to do.

## Weekly — 45 minutes (Monday)
1. **Pipeline → Stale only.** Move each deal forward, follow up, or close it.
2. **Growth.** Act on the recommended actions. Check which vertical, market and channel is replying. Fewer than 10 contacted means too early to judge.
3. **Finance.**
   - Add a record when a client confirms. Mark it **Sent** when the invoice goes out.
   - **Record payment** when money lands.
   - Chase anything **Overdue**.
4. **LinkedIn → People you already know.** Pick 5 people at active prospects and press **Draft message**. Send the drafts you like.
5. **Past relationships → Worth reconnecting.** Restart 3 real conversations. Use **Add to CRM** for the useful ones and **Not relevant** for the rest.
6. **Reports.** Read the weekly review.

## Monthly — 1 hour
1. **System costs.** Fill every UNKNOWN cost and renewal date from invoices. Decide whether to keep each paid service.
2. **Markets.** Choose where to push next month: Europe → Egypt, GCC → Egypt / Europe, or Egypt → International.
3. **LinkedIn.** Re-import your connections export (Me → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data → Connections).
4. **Companies with unknown country.** Set the country from real evidence (Edit details). Never guess.
5. **Review:**
   - collected vs won revenue and the pipeline;
   - vertical and market performance (Growth);
   - reactivation results.
6. **Stop list.** Decide what to stop doing.

## What the words mean
- **Ready outreach:** qualified outreach prepared for you to review. Nothing is sent until you act.
- **Reconnect:** a previous relationship worth restarting rather than cold outreach.
- **Emailed before:** NOYA Gmail has a real thread with them. HQ will never send them a cold introduction.
- **Pipeline:** potential opportunities being pursued. It is not revenue.
- **Collected:** money actually recorded as received.

## Rules that never change
- **Nothing is sent without you.** Email goes through a Gmail draft you send. LinkedIn and Instagram are always sent by you. Auto-reply is off.
- **No paid tool, upgrade or credit without your approval.** HQ shows the cost first.
- **Pipeline is an estimate, not revenue.** Collected is money received. Currencies are never added together.
- **UNKNOWN means unknown**, never zero.
- **Your LinkedIn password or session is never asked for or stored.**

## Automation levels
1. **Prepare.** NOYA researches, drafts and recommends; you decide and send. This covers most of NOYA today.
2. **Act with approval.** NOYA acts after one click. Today: Approve → Gmail draft.
3. **Act alone.** Only internal bookkeeping (logging replies, booking follow-ups). Nothing client-facing moves to level 3 without your explicit approval.
